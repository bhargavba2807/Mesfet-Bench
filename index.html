Gradual-channel current follows the Shockley JFET solution with the Schottky built-in potential in place of a junction built-in voltage. Velocity saturation is applied as a source-end current ceiling, I = qN_D·v_sat·Z·(a − W_s), which is where the 0.5 µm figures stop agreeing with the long-channel algebra. Series resistances are solved self-consistently, so Rs both pushes the knee out and degrades gm by 1/(1 + g_m·R_s). Set them to zero and the knee collapses to about EcL ≈ 0.1 V — the intrinsic channel really does saturate that fast, and almost all of a measured knee voltage is parasitic. Gate forward conduction above roughly +0.6 V, substrate leakage, impact ionization and the velocity overshoot peculiar to GaAs are left out. Source on GitHub
